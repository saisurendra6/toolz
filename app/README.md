# 🚀 App Installation Guide

Follow these steps to get started:

1. 📥 **Download** the app from the **`/app`** folder.  
2. 🛡️ **Pause Google Play Protect** during installation.  
   - Since this app isn’t on the Play Store, Play Protect might block it.  
3. ✅ **Re-enable Play Protect** once installation is complete.  

✨ That’s it — you’re all set!  
Enjoy the app and stay **productive** 💡💻🔥
